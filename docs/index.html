THE DIRECTION

Not another place
to lose yourself.

Less friction between the things you do.
More room for what makes you, you.

01

Room for every side of you.

Named profiles and side-by-side identities are a core goal. Personal browsing, creative projects and different site accounts should feel distinct, without a maze of windows.

Identity-aware browsing
02

Keep the good stuff close.

A local-first collection for websites, media references, creators and characters. Organize favorites with source links, groups and search, instead of another forgotten bookmark pile.

Your personal collection
03

A little more personality.
A lot less baggage.

Expressive themes and optional tools, with a leaner experience as the goal. Technology earns its place through real testing, not a trend or a marketing benchmark.

Customizable by design

VERIFICATION CHECKPOINT

Tested contracts.
Native acceptance pending.

At candidate 362fb016, 15 logic tests and syntax checks passed on Windows and Linux. Preview packages passed a file allowlist and integrity check.

Actual GUI behavior, session isolation, media playback, security and performance still need native acceptance. No claim of being faster than other browsers.
